How DielFold Works

What is DielFold?

DielFold takes a normal 24-hour day and gently slices your waking time into slightly shorter hours of 53m 20s each. The minutes you save quietly collect into two built-in reset hours during the day—without cutting a single minute of sleep.

How Long Is Your Day? (26 Apparent Hours)

18 waking hrs (53m 20s)
8 sleep (60m)
18 waking hours (53m 20s each) + 8 real sleep hours (60m each) = 26 apparent hours in a 24-hour day.
Prefer an even gentler pace? The Stealth preset (17/16x) gives 25 apparent hours (17 waking + 8 sleep).

Why It Works

  • No sleep disruption. Your 8-hour sleep window runs at normal 1.00x clock speed, keeping your internal biological rhythm anchored to daylight.
  • Subtle pacing, not rushing. Shaving 6 minutes 40 seconds off each hour is small enough to feel natural while quietly nudging tasks to finish instead of dragging on.
  • Two guilt-free pause hours ("Seams"). Saved time gathers into a Midday Seam during your afternoon energy dip (~2:00 PM) and a Twilight Seam right before bed. At 3:00 PM and bedtime, DielFold snaps cleanly back in sync with normal wall time.

How to Use It Daily

  1. Set your usual wake-up time in Settings (S) so your 16-hour waking window and Seams line up with your routine.
  2. Press H for Focus Mode while working so the normal wall clock fades out and you stop converting times in your head.
  3. Pin your next real-world deadline under Tools (M)—DielFold stays completely out of sight until 10 minutes before you need to wrap up.
